Frequently Asked Questions

What is your MOQ?

Our MOQ is 1pcs for trial order. You can place small test orders to check quality before bulk purchase.

Can I customize logo on pet products?

Yes. We support custom printed or embossed LOGO on most pet items. Send us your logo file for quotation.

Do you accept OEM & ODM orders?

Absolutely. We can develop new products based on your drawings, samples or design ideas.

What file format do you need for custom logo?

Vector files such as AI, PDF, EPS are preferred. PNG with transparent background is also acceptable.

How long is production & delivery time?

For ready stock items, we can ship quickly. Custom OEM orders usually take 7–20 days, depends on quantity and customization.

How can I get price list?

Fill our quote form or contact us via WhatsApp, tell your product requirements and quantity, we will send you the quotation quickly.
